How to check redirects and broken links for thousands of URLs at once

After a site migration, a rebrand or a CMS clean-up, the question is always the same: do the old URLs still land somewhere sensible? Clicking through a few hundred links by hand misses the chains, loops and quiet 404s that cost you traffic.

What you get

For each URL, the Bulk URL Status & Redirect Checker follows every redirect and returns:

  • statusCode and category: the final answer (ok, client-error, server-error, redirect-loop, timeout, unreachable...)
  • finalUrl: where the URL really ends up (great for expanding short links)
  • redirectChain: every hop with its status code (301, 302, 307, 308) and time
  • httpsUpgrade and crossDomainRedirect: whether http went to https, and whether the URL left the original domain
  • title, contentType and responseTimeMs
  • securityHeaders: HSTS, CSP, X-Frame-Options and three more, plus a 0-6 score

Steps

  1. Open the tool on Apify and paste your URLs into URLs, one per line (up to 20,000 per run).
  2. Run it. It checks several sites in parallel, but only one request at a time per site, so a few hundred URLs on one domain take a few minutes.
  3. Export as CSV or Excel. Filter redirectCount above 1 to find chains to flatten, and ok = false to find what's broken.

A migration checklist

  • Every old URL should return one 301 to its new page: redirectCount = 1, statusCode = 200.
  • redirect-loop and too-many-redirects rows are urgent: browsers show an error.
  • A 302 where you meant a 301 shows up in redirectChain[].statusCode.
  • Many old URLs pointing at the home page usually means a catch-all redirect that search engines treat as a soft 404.
